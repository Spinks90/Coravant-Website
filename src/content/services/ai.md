AI has been sold hard for three years, and most businesses we speak to have seen a demo, been impressed, and then struggled to name a job it would actually do for them on Monday morning.

The uses that pay for themselves are usually dull. Reading a supplier invoice and getting it into the accounts system without anyone typing it. Sorting three hundred inbound emails into the right queue. Pulling the delivery date out of a customer's purchase order. Answering a stock question at eleven at night that would otherwise wait until morning.

## How we approach it

We look for tasks that are high volume, low judgement, and currently done by a person reading a screen. We prove the value on a small slice of real work before anyone commits to a platform, and we build in a way that lets a human check the output.

## What you get

An honest assessment of where AI helps you and where it does not, then a working system for the parts where it does.
