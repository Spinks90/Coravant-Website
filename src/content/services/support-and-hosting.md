A system that nobody maintains is a system quietly going wrong. Certificates expire, disks fill, integrations break after a supplier changes an endpoint, and the person who knew how it all worked left two years ago.

## How we approach it

We host on infrastructure sized to the job, monitor it so that we usually know before you do, and keep it patched. When something does break you speak to a developer who can read the code, not a call centre that raises a ticket.

We also take over systems built by other people. If the previous developer has gone quiet, that is one of the commonest reasons businesses call us.

## What you get

Managed hosting, monitoring and backups, agreed response times, and a named team who know your system.
