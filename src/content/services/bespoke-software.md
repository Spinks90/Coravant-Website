Every business ends up with a job that off-the-shelf software will not do. A pricing rule nobody else has. A production process built over twenty years. A spreadsheet that three people understand and everyone else is frightened of.

That is where bespoke software earns its money. Not a grand replacement of everything you own, but one well-made tool that removes a bottleneck you have been working around for years.

## How we approach it

We start with the process, not the technology. We watch how the work is done now, find where the time actually goes, and write down what the software has to achieve. Then we quote a fixed price against that, so you are agreeing to an outcome rather than an open-ended day rate.

Builds are delivered in stages. You see working software within weeks, not at the end of a six-month silence, and you can change your mind while changing your mind is still cheap.

## What you get

Software that matches how your business actually runs, documentation your own IT people can follow, the source code in your ownership, and a team that answers the phone after go-live.
