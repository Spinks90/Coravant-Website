  - q: Should we buy a CRM or build one?

A CRM only works if the people who sell actually use it. Most do not, because the system was set up to produce management reports rather than to make a salesperson's day easier.

## How we approach it

We start from the sales team's side of the desk. What would make logging a call faster than not logging it? Usually the answer is connecting the CRM to the systems that already hold the information: the ERP that knows what the customer bought, the website that knows what they looked at, the accounts package that knows whether they pay on time.

We implement and customise the mainstream platforms, we integrate them with what you already run, and where a business genuinely has a sales process that no product fits, we build it.

## What you get

A single, current view of every customer, quotes that do not go missing, and forecasts based on what is actually in the system rather than what somebody remembered on a Friday.
