An ERP system is supposed to be the single place your orders, stock, purchasing and finance all meet. In practice most of them fit the business that bought them about eighty per cent of the way, and everybody quietly builds spreadsheets to cover the rest.

Those spreadsheets are where the errors live, and where the time goes.

## How we approach it

We have spent decades inside SAP ERP, including the parts most consultancies will not touch: ABAP development, SAP UI5 screens, OData services, S/4HANA migrations and the integration layer that connects SAP to everything else you run. We do the same work on other ERP platforms, and we are the only UK partner for DataXstream's OMS+ order management software.

The question we start with is never which module to buy. It is which part of the day is being wasted, and whether the system you already own can be made to do it.

## What you get

Fewer manual workarounds, an ERP that matches your actual processes, and a straight answer about whether an upgrade or a migration is genuinely worth the disruption.
